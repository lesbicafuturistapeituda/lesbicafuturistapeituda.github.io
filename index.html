🎯 What is Geny Assistant?

Geny Assistant is a revolutionary virtual assistant designed to work completely offline on your Android device. Unlike popular assistants that send your data to cloud servers, Geny processes everything right on your phone, ensuring your conversations, commands, and personal information never leave your device.

Whether you're tech-savvy or a beginner, Geny makes it easy to control your device, get answers, manage tasks, and more — all with a simple, intuitive interface.

💡 Key Benefit: Your data stays yours. No internet connection needed after download.

✨ Amazing Features

🔒 100% Private & Local-First

All processing happens on your device. No cloud, no servers, no tracking. Perfect for sensitive tasks.

⚙️ Full System Control

Works with both rooted and non-rooted devices. Control apps, settings, and more with voice or text commands.

🎤 Voice & Text Input

Speak naturally or type your commands. Powered by fast, on-device speech recognition.

🧠 Advanced AI Models

Uses cutting-edge LLM technology (Llama.cpp, Whisper.cpp) to understand and execute complex requests.

🎨 Customizable with Lua

Extend functionality with easy-to-use Lua scripts. Create custom commands and automation workflows.

🚀 Fast & Lightweight

Optimized with Rust and Kotlin for smooth, responsive performance without draining your battery.

🤔 Frequently Asked Questions

Is Geny Assistant really free?

Yes! Geny Assistant is completely free and open-source. No hidden costs, no subscriptions, no premium tiers.

Does it work without internet?

Absolutely! Once you download and install the app, everything works offline. That's the beauty of local-first processing.

Can I use it on non-rooted phones?

Yes! Geny Assistant works on both rooted and non-rooted devices. Root access simply unlocks extra system-level controls.

How is my data protected?

Since everything runs on your device, your data never leaves your phone. There's no server to hack, no cloud to breach.
